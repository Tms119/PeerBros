# PEERBROS

## The Provocation Strategy

### Objective

Turn PeerBros from a generic software agency into a recognizable movement.

The goal is not for everyone to like PeerBros.

The goal is for a business owner to see a PeerBros post and immediately think:

> “These guys are attacking people like me.”

Then:

> “Wait. They're actually right.”

Then:

> “What exactly do these guys do?”

That sequence creates attention, conversation and eventually demand.

---

PeerBros currently sells too many things:

• Websites
• Ecommerce
• Automation
• Custom web apps

• Custom solutions

That creates a service company.

Service companies are forgettable.

# NARRATIVE 01

# “YOUR BUSINESS IS A FRAUD.”

## The villain

### Fake Business Success

The modern business world has convinced owners that:

More employees = bigger business.

More revenue = better business.

More meetings = productivity.

More software = sophistication.

More complexity = scale.

More hours = hard work.

PeerBros attacks this belief.

The argument:

> **A lot of businesses aren't actually scalable businesses.**
>
> They're piles of manual processes held together by employees.

A $2M company with 30 people manually moving information around isn't necessarily more sophisticated than a $500k company with five people and excellent systems.

Revenue can hide operational stupidity.

Headcount can hide broken processes.

Busy employees can hide terrible systems.

That is the provocation.

---

## The core accusation

# “You didn't build a business. You built a job factory.”

This is the headline.

It will piss some owners off.

Good.

Then explain:

> You hired someone to manage the spreadsheet.
>
> Then someone to update the CRM.
>
> Then someone to follow up with leads.
>
> Then someone to prepare reports.
>
> Then someone to check whether everyone did their work.
>
> Then someone to manage all those people.
>
> Congratulations.
>
> You built a bureaucracy.

The solution is not “fire everyone.”

The solution is:

**Find the work that exists only because your systems suck. Then eliminate it.**

---

## Brand belief

### Revenue is not operational excellence.

PeerBros believes a business should be measured by:

• How little pointless work it requires
• How much output each person can create
• How many processes run without intervention
• How quickly information moves
• How little management overhead exists

## The worldview

PeerBros believes:

> **Humans should not compete with software at being software.**

A person can think.

A person can negotiate.

A person can sell.

A person can create.

A person can make decisions.

A person should not spend four hours copying rows between spreadsheets.

Current discussions on X and Reddit are moving in exactly this direction. The strongest arguments are increasingly about using AI and automation to give existing employees leverage rather than simply buying another tool. One recent X discussion described saving 8 to 10 hours a week by automating repetitive workflows, while Reddit agency owners are discussing the pressure for smaller teams and faster delivery as AI coding becomes more capable.
